Privacy policy

Claude Ads Automation. Last updated October 20, 2026.

Claude Ads Automation (“the app”) is an internal tool used by a small team to work with Google Ads accounts they already have access to. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

What data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app requests access to the Google Ads API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords). This lets it read and, when you choose to, change data in the Google Ads accounts your Google account can access, such as campaigns, ad groups, ads, budgets and performance metrics.

The app does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Google Ads data is used only to produce reports and to carry out campaign actions that you start yourself. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to managing your own Google Ads accounts.

Where the data is stored

The app runs on your own computer. Your OAuth credentials and refresh token are stored locally in a configuration file on your machine. The app has no central server and does not keep a copy of your data anywhere else.

If you use an AI assistant (such as Claude) to run the app’s scripts, the data those scripts return is processed by that assistant under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties. Data is only shared with the people inside the team who already have access to the same Google Ads accounts.

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app’s access at any time on your Google Account permissions page. To remove locally stored credentials, delete the configuration file from your computer.
