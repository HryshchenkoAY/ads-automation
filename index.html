Claude Ads Automation

Internal tool for a small marketing team

Claude Ads Automation is an internal tool that connects to the Google Ads API so that authorized team members can pull performance reports and manage their own advertising campaigns from scripts running on their computers.

It is not a public product and is not offered to anyone outside the team. Access is limited to people who have been granted access to the relevant Google Ads accounts.
